θAB ≈ 29.25° (≤ 30°) Disagree

The inclination of edge $AB$ to the horizontal ground is $\arcsin\left(\frac{h_B - h_A}{AB}\right) \approx 29.25^\circ$. Since $29.25^\circ \le 30^\circ$, the claim is incorrect (Do not agree).

Dihedral Inclination φ ≈ 54.38° c(ii)

1. In $\triangle ADC$, $\angle ADC \approx 100.55^\circ$.

2. Azimuth difference on ground: $\cos \Delta\alpha \approx -0.600 \implies \Delta\alpha \approx 126.84^\circ$.

3. Angle between card plane $ABCD$ and the horizontal ground:

cos φ = |nz| ≈ 0.5823 &implies; φ ≈ 54.38° (or 54.4°)

Vertex C is closer to Ground c(i)

• Distance from $A$ to ground: $h_A = 18\sin 39^\circ \approx 11.33\text{ cm}$.

• Distance from $C$ to ground: $h_C = 12\sin 23^\circ \approx 4.69\text{ cm}$.

Since $4.69\text{ cm} < 11.33\text{ cm}$, vertex $C$ is closer.

∠ADC ≈ 100.55° (a)

$AC = \sqrt{28^2 + 25^2 - 2(28)(25)\cos 52^\circ} \approx 23.39\text{ cm}$.
$\cos \angle ADC = \frac{18^2 + 12^2 - 23.39^2}{2(18)(12)} \approx -0.1830 \implies \angle ADC \approx 100.55^\circ$.

Area(ΔACD) First Increases, Then Decreases (b)

As $\angle ABC$ drops from $52^\circ$ to $32^\circ$, $AC$ decreases, causing $\angle ADC$ to decrease from $100.55^\circ$ past $90^\circ$ to $55.24^\circ$. Since $\sin \angle ADC$ peaks at $90^\circ$, the area first increases to $108\text{ cm}^2$ and then decreases.
